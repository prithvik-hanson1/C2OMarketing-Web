About C20

Curiosity.
Craft.
Practical thinking.

We’re a student-led digital solutions and marketing agency in Kerala, bringing creative and technical interests to the everyday needs of smaller businesses.

Considered solutions.
Human conversations.

We help independent businesses build a stronger identity, establish a professional digital presence and connect with the customers who matter.

We work through defined projects: understand the problem, agree the scope and price, create the solution and deliver it with care. We value clear communication, useful work and honest expectations.
