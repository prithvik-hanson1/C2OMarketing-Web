Kerala food · Irinjalakuda

Swaad Thattukada

Rooted in Kerala's homestyle food, Swaad Thattukada highlights local flavours, kanji and house specials. Its menu also features familiar favourites such as porotta, puttu, curries and biryani.

C20 has partnered with Swaad to support its digital presence. We're building the work around the business's character and the people it serves, with more details to share as the collaboration develops.

Tea Time describes itself as a place for “brewing moments of wellness” in Kolothumpady, Irinjalakuda. Its Instagram features tea and drink specials alongside burgers, sandwiches, noodles and desserts.
