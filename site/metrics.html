Examples, not promises

Different goals
leave different clues.

We choose a small set of signals with the business, then interpret them in context.

Discovery

Are more of the right people finding you?

Possible signals include profile visits, local search visibility, or relevant reach.

Attention

Does the message hold someone's interest?

Useful clues might be watch time, saves, repeat visits, or thoughtful replies.

Intent

Do people take a meaningful next step?

Depending on the business, that could mean a call, enquiry, booking, or visit.

Outcome

Is the work serving the business goal?

The business outcome matters most. Online activity is context, not proof on its own.
