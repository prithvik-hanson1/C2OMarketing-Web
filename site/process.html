Delivery is more than a download

Ready to use.

Payment terms, schedule, support and any applicable revision time limits are agreed in your proposal.

Planning and design-direction revisions are free within the original agreed scope. One free minor revision is included after handover. Extra deliverables, major changes and project restarts are quoted separately.

Useful measures, where they fit

Look for signals
that matter.

When tracking is feasible and included in the scope, we can agree measures such as website visits, form submissions, WhatsApp clicks, booking enquiries, campaign responses, Google Business Profile interactions or conversions through trackable links.

We agree a baseline, review the relevant signals and explain their limits. These are possible measures, not claims of client results. Reporting and any recurring evaluation are scoped to the project.

Before we begin

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Are your prices fixed?

Prices are indicative and negotiable. Final quotations depend on project scope and requirements.

Do I have to pay for monthly services?

No. Most C20 services are project-based. Ongoing maintenance, hosting management or recurring reporting is optional and separately scoped.

Is printing included?

Digital design only. Printing, physical production, materials, delivery and external vendor charges are separate.

Can I request revisions?

Planning and design-direction revisions are free within the original agreed scope. One free minor revision is included after handover. Extra deliverables, major changes and project restarts are quoted separately. Website bug fixes needed to meet the original agreed scope are part of completing that work, not paid design revisions. Payment terms, schedule, support and any applicable revision time limits are agreed in your proposal.

Do business-card packages include multiple concepts?

Each business-card package includes two initial design options. You choose one direction for the final card; this does not include two independently finalized projects. This entitlement applies to the three card packages, not automatically to bundled services or websites.

Do you offer custom solutions?

Yes, depending on feasibility and requirements. Tell us what you need and we will discuss an appropriate scope.

Can you host and maintain my website?

We can discuss hosting and maintenance requirements and quote them separately. Ownership, access and support responsibilities are agreed in the proposal.

Do you guarantee increased sales?

No. We focus on useful solutions and measurable outcomes where tracking is feasible.

How long does a project take?

Each project has an agreed schedule based on its scope, content, review stages and dependencies. We do not promise one delivery time for every project.

What happens after I enquire?

We review your requirements, clarify the brief and discuss an appropriate proposal. Sending an enquiry does not commit you to a project.
